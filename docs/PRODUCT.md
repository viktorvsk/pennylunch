# Product

## Register

product

## Users

PennyLunch is for home cooks deciding what to make for dinner with ingredients they already have. They are usually in a practical, time-sensitive workflow: enter available ingredients, scan recipe matches, compare time and category, then open a recipe that looks feasible.

## Product Purpose

PennyLunch helps users find relevant recipes from their at-home ingredients. Success means the interface makes ingredient entry fast, recipe results scannable, and the next cooking choice clear without exposing import, indexing, or implementation details.

## Brand Personality

Practical, warm, and focused. The product can carry a little food personality through the pen mascot and ingredient moments, but the core UI should feel like a reliable cooking tool rather than a marketing surface.

## Anti-references

Avoid landing-page hero treatment, decorative card grids, oversized illustration-first screens, generic SaaS gradients, and novelty controls that slow down recipe search. Do not let the mascot or iconography compete with ingredients, recipes, or filter controls.

## Design Principles

1. Keep recipe discovery task-first.
2. Use warmth as seasoning, not decoration.
3. Make ingredient state visible and reversible.
4. Preserve dense, readable scanning across mobile and desktop.
5. Prefer familiar Rails, Turbo, and Basecoat patterns over custom interaction vocabulary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast, focus visibility, keyboard operation, and reduced-motion compatibility. Ingredient and filter workflows must remain understandable without relying on color alone.
